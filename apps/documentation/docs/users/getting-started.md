BudgetBuddy exists as a hosted service at [app.budget-buddy.de](https://app.budget-buddy.de) and as a self-hosted web app. This guide applies to both; only the address and the enabled sign-in methods can differ, depending on how the instance is configured.

## Create an account

1. Open the web app and choose **Sign up**.
2. Register with your name, email address, and a password, or use GitHub or Google if the operator enabled social sign-in.
3. After signing up, a verification email is sent. Open the link in it to verify your address. Unverified accounts see a reminder in the profile settings with a button to resend the mail.

## Sign in and password reset

- Sign in with your email and password on the **Sign in** page.
- If you forgot your password, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. You will receive an email with a reset link.
- Signing out ends the current session. Active sessions can be reviewed and revoked under [Account](/users/account).

## Set up your first data

Transactions require a category and a payment method, so create those first:

1. **Create categories** — for example _Groceries_, _Rent_, or _Salary_. See [Categories](/users/categories).
2. **Create payment methods** — for example your bank account, cash, or a credit card. See [Payment methods](/users/payment-methods).
3. **Record your first transaction** — date, receiver, amount, category, and payment method. See [Transactions](/users/transactions).
4. **Optional: add recurring payments** — for subscriptions and regular bills. BudgetBuddy then creates the matching transactions automatically. See [Recurring payments](/users/recurring-payments).
5. **Optional: set budgets** — monthly spending or income goals across categories. See [Budgets](/users/budgets).
6. **Explore the dashboard** — overview, budget monitor, and insights. See [Dashboard and insights](/users/dashboard).

You can navigate quickly at any time with the command palette in the header.
