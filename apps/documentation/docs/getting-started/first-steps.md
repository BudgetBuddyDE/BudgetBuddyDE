## Recommended Order

1. Create one or more [categories](../user-guide/categories.md).
2. Configure your [payment methods](../user-guide/payment-methods.md).
3. Record your first [transactions](../user-guide/transactions.md).
4. Define [budgets](../user-guide/budgets.md) for important categories.
5. Add [recurring payments](../user-guide/recurring-payments.md).
6. Review your progress in the [dashboard and analytics](../user-guide/analytics.md).

The available features and their current development status are listed in the [project status](../project/roadmap.md).
