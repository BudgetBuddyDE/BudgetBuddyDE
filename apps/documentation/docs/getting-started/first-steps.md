## Recommended Order

1. Create one or more [categories](/user-guide/categories).
2. Configure your [payment methods](/user-guide/payment-methods).
3. Record your first [transactions](/user-guide/transactions).
4. Define [budgets](/user-guide/budgets) for important categories.
5. Add [recurring payments](/user-guide/recurring-payments).
6. Review your progress in the [dashboard and analytics](/user-guide/analytics).

The available features and their current development status are listed in the [project status](/project/roadmap).
