The dashboard area has three views: the overview, the budget monitor, and insights.

## Overview

The dashboard start page summarizes your current financial situation:

- Key figures for the current month, including paid and upcoming expenses and income, and the amount still free.
- Upcoming payments, combining future-dated transactions and the next occurrences of active recurring payments.
- Recent transactions for a quick check of the latest activity.

Clicking through the cards leads to filtered lists, for example the transactions behind a figure.

## Budget monitor

The **Budget** view shows your monthly goals from [Budgets](/users/budgets):

- Each budget with its progress against the current month.
- A radar chart comparing all spending goals.
- Charts for recurring payments, income, and expenses by category.

## Insights

The **Insights** view looks at longer time ranges:

- Historical balance, showing how your balance developed over months.
- Category breakdowns for income and expenses.
- Comparison charts across categories and periods.
