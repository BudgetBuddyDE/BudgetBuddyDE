Account settings live under **Settings → Profile**. The page shows your profile header with avatar, your editable profile data, your linked accounts, your active sessions, and data management.

## Profile

- **Avatar** — upload an image via the profile header.
- **Name** — editable at any time.
- **Email** — changing your address requires confirmation: BudgetBuddy sends a verification link to your current email address, and the change only takes effect after you open it.
- **Email verification** — if your address is not verified yet, a warning with a **Verify email** button is shown. The same reminder pattern applies after registering.

Use **Edit** to unlock the fields, then **Save changes** or **Discard**.

## Linked accounts

If the operator enabled social sign-in, you can connect and disconnect GitHub and Google accounts. At least one sign-in method must remain linked; the last one cannot be removed.

## Sessions

The session list shows all devices and browsers that are currently signed in, with details about each session. Use **Revoke sessions** to sign out everywhere else. This is the recommended action after losing a device or suspecting misuse.

## Password

There is no separate change-password form inside the app. Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page to receive a reset link. Resetting your password revokes all existing sessions.

## Delete account

The **Delete account** button starts a verified deletion:

1. Confirm the dialog with your password.
2. BudgetBuddy sends a final confirmation email to your address.
3. Open the link in that email to complete the deletion.

> **Warning:** Account deletion permanently removes your data. Export anything you want to keep first. See [Export and import data](/users/data-management).
